Bihari Farm Heritage

Every product tells a story of local mud, holy water, and generational knowledge. Learn about the legacy of our organic crops.

Muzaffarpur Legacy

The Rose-Scented Shahi Litchi

Muzaffarpur's soil is uniquely high in calcium, fed by Gangetic silt. This matches the climate perfectly to produce Shahi Litchi, known for its rose-like aroma, translucent flesh, and intense juiciness.

Bhagalpur Treasure

The Golden Jardalu Mango

A GI-tagged royal mango lineage. Cultivated in the fertile Bhagalpur floodplains, the Jardalu Mango is easily identified by its light yellow, soft skin and uniquely sweet, earthy scent that signals summer's arrival.

Mithila Superfood

Mithila Makhana: Water Lilies

Grown in natural ponds of Mithila, Makhana harvesting is an artisanal, backbreaking process. Popped by hand over high flames, these white foxnuts are premium, highly nutritious superfoods rich in history.
