Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about harvest schedules, order tracking, or delivery timelines?

How do I track my live order dispatch?

Once your order is packed by our farmer cooperative, an SMS and WhatsApp alert will be sent with your unique Tracking ID (e.g., KK-8924-9102). You can also click the "Track Order" button on our header menu at any time to view real-time status.

How are fresh fruits like Shahi Litchi preserved during transit?

Litchis and mangoes are picked early morning before sunrise, pre-cooled in shaded orchard sheds, and packed in ventilated insulation boxes. They are transported via Express Air Cargo to reach metro hubs within 24-36 hours.

Can I schedule delivery for Chhath Puja offerings?

Yes! Select the "Festival Freight" tier at checkout and enter your preferred delivery date. Our team schedules the dispatch so your sacred bamboo soop, jaggery, and Gangajal arrive 1-2 days prior to the festival start date.
